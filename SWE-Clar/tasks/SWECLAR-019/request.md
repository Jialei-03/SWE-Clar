Extend the supplied working Python HTTP/SQLite/browser application. It already supports synthetic account login, mandatory name/photo profiles, actual PNG upload and display, waypoint creation, ordered route creation, title filtering and a schematic SVG map. Complete alphabetical route ordering and personal pins, explicit touring-status submission, safe waypoint coordinate editing, and reliable comments with retries.

The local development environment and synthetic fixtures are prepared. Work in `/workspace`; no customer accounts or external deployment are required.
