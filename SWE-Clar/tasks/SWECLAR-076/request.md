# Portal training arena — browser adaptation

Complete process_portal_event(state,event) in solution.py while preserving actual native WebGL3D rendering and interaction. The supplied independent scene provides room/cockpit portal controls, continuous or single-step asteroid motion, weapon/shield controls, visible enemies and shots, wave spawning and restart feedback. This is a small browser adaptation of a Unity/Quest gameplay scenario; it does not execute the original game, headset tracking, AR room scans or proprietary assets.

The local development environment and synthetic fixtures are prepared. Work in `/workspace`; no customer accounts or external deployment are required.
